## The three Pro features that matter most

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verified crawler identity">
    A user agent is a string anyone can send. Free logging tells you something claimed to be GPTBot; Pro confirms it by reverse DNS or the vendor's published IP ranges. Without that, your AI numbers can be inflated by anything that wants to look like a crawler.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI referral tracking">
    A crawler reading a page proves it is reachable. A person arriving from a ChatGPT or Perplexity answer proves it was cited. That second number is the one worth optimizing against, and most analytics bury it under generic referral traffic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic redirects on slug change">
    The single most common way a site loses traffic is renaming a published page and forgetting the redirect. Pro writes the 301 the moment you save, and repoints the existing rule rather than building a chain if you rename twice.
  </Step>
</Steps>
